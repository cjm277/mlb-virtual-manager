# MLB Virtual Manager: tester guide

Thanks for trying this! You're the manager of a big-league club. The app has an AI coaching staff that prepares your game plan. Think out loud as you go: what you expect, what surprises you, what you'd do next. There are no wrong answers. If something is confusing, that's a problem with the app, not with you.

**📷 = the person running the session takes a screenshot here.**

## Your tasks

1. **Pick your team, an opponent, and a game.** Any team you like. Games from the 2026 season or the published 2027 schedule both work. 📷
2. **Build the game plan.** Watch the staff work for about a minute. What do you think is happening? 📷
3. **Read the lineup card.** Would you start these nine in this order? Find one player's reason and tell us whether it convinces you. 📷
4. **Find out who the other team will probably start on the mound, and how sure the staff is.**
5. **Look at the pitching plan.** Who starts for you, and who comes in if it's close late?
6. **Change something with the dropdowns.** For example, swap two players or change a position. 📷
7. **Change something by chatting with the assistant coach.** Try something like "I don't like [player] in this matchup" or "get [player] in there." 📷
8. **Download the packets** and open one file. Who is each packet for? 📷

If you have time:

9. **Build a second plan for a different game.** Was it easier the second time?

## Afterwards

Fill in the short feedback form. It takes about 3 minutes.
