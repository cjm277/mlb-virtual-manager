**What none of them does, and this app does:**
- **Data and video tools** (TruMedia, Synergy, Inside Edge, Savant) give a staff the information. People still assemble the plan: the lineup, the defensive alignment, the starter, the bullpen by game situation, both scouting reports, and a briefing for each group.
- **General chatbots** produce the whole plan, but from no verified data. Nothing checks the numbers or the rules, and they have no idea who is injured or rested.
- **This app** has a staff of agents produce one consistent plan end to end from the data. Code checks every rule and every quoted number. The lineup and the starter come from a win model chosen on held-out data, not tradition. Edits in plain English are re-checked before saving, and each group gets its own packet.
